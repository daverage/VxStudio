VxCleanup is legacy and being archived. VxClarity (Speech Clarity) and VxRefine (ToneRefine) are the active replacements.

**Why:** Cleanup is being superseded by purpose-specific products with cleaner separation of concerns.

**How to apply:** Don't spend effort improving VxCleanup. When referencing shelf/corrective DSP patterns, use Clarity and Refine as the canonical examples, not Cleanup.
