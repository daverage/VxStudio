What Each Plugin Does

Quick reference notes for the full suite

Tone

Perceptual bass and treble shaping for broad tonal balance moves, with a neutral center and stronger edge behavior.

Cleanup

Corrective cleanup for mud, harshness, breaths, plosives, and sibilance using confidence-aware shared analysis.

Denoiser

Broadband denoise for hiss, fan noise, and steady background beds, with less hidden restoration at strong settings.

Deverb

Room-tail reduction tuned to act on late ambience more intentionally instead of flattening the whole source.

Finish

Final polish, recovery lift, and safer shared finishing with true-peak-aware behavior.

OptoComp

Smooth opto-style levelling built on the shared finishing core, with wider output authority than the earlier polite build.

Proximity

Source-aware close-mic modeling for intimacy, body, and air, with smoothing to avoid pop-prone retuning.

Subtract

Learn a repeatable noise profile, then subtract it with smarter trust when the learned bed no longer matches the input.

Leveler

Adaptive levelling with a clearer split between vocal riding and whole-mix level control.

DeepFilterNet

ML-backed denoise for heavier or more complex interference, with runtime guard behavior shaped by artifact risk.

Rebalance

Confidence-driven source-family rebalance for vocals, drums, bass, guitar, and other mix content without stems.

Studio Analyser

Track-local chain analysis that follows only the active upstream path feeding the instance you are inspecting.
